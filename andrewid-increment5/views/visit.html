Plan Your Visit

We are located at 5000 Forbes Ave, Pittsburgh, PA 15213, just a short walk from the Carnegie Mellon University campus. Visitors can easily access the museum via public transportation, with several bus lines stopping nearby and parking available in the adjacent lot. We look forward to welcoming you to MonoMuse for an unforgettable experience at the intersection of art and technology.

Know Before You Go

  • Fully wheelchair accessible with elevators and accessible restrooms
  • Free Wi-Fi available throughout the museum
  • Coat check and lockers available; large bags may need to be stored
  • Plan 1-2 hours for your visit; check for timed exhibits
